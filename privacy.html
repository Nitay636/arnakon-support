Privacy Policy

This policy covers the apps Arnakon and Arnakon Trip. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

The expenses, categories, incomes, trips, notes and receipt photos you enter, plus the names you choose for people in your household or trip. If you allow it, the location where a payment was made.

How it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and is linked to an anonymous ID, not to your name, email or phone number. It is visible only to people who have your household or trip invite code.

Receipt scanning

When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic to extract items and amounts. We do not use it for anything else.

Camera, photos and location

The camera and photo library are used only to scan receipts you choose. Location is used only to save where a payment was made, and only when you allow it.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of your data with third parties other than the Google services above.

Deleting your data

Delete items, trips or the household inside the app, or contact us to have your data removed.
