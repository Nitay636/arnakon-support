Arnakon & Arnakon Trip

Arnakon tracks household expenses that everyone in your home shares. Arnakon Trip splits and tracks expenses on a trip with friends. Both read receipts (including Hebrew) automatically.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open a support request and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I add someone to my household or trip? Share the invite code from Settings (household) or the trip screen. They enter it in their own app.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up. Your data is tied to an anonymous ID and shared only with people who have your invite code.

Is my receipt photo stored? It is sent to Google's Gemini service only to read the items, and the result is saved with your expense.

How do I delete my data? Delete the expenses, trips or household inside the app, or contact us and we will remove it.
